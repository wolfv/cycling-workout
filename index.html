Generate a workout with AI

Copy this template into your preferred assistant, describe your goals, then import its JSON response. 

Create a cycling workout for [goal], lasting [minutes], for [experience level]. Include warmup, recoveries, and cooldown. Return valid JSON only, with this structure:
{"name":"My workout","ftp":200,"intervals":[{"name":"Warmup","type":"warmup","duration":300,"powerType":"relative","percentage":50}]}
Duration is in seconds. Use powerType "relative" with percentage, "absolute" with power in watts, or "ramp" with percentageLow and percentageHigh. Interval types: warmup, endurance, tempo, threshold, vo2max, cooldown.
